Data and method

The viewer shows magnetospheric current paths traced through frozen GAMERA snapshots, together with REMIX ionospheric field-aligned current. Ten times are sampled evenly from 2 to 24 model hours, using the nearest saved output (within about 30 seconds). The first two hours are omitted as model burn-in. Arrows show conventional-current direction; their animation speed is illustrative, while model time remains fixed.

Current paths

The curves integrate dr/ds = J/|J| from GAMERA's Jx, Jy and Jz on all 104,206 cell centres in the extracted volume. Linear tetrahedral interpolation and RK4 with a 0.12 Earth-radius step define the paths. Eight spatially distributed seeds per family are displayed; all traced paths are retained in the HDF5 product.

Colors identify seed families, not a unique decomposition of the entire current system. Region 1-like seeds select outward dusk / inward dawn current at high latitude on the inner MHD shell. Region 2-like seeds select the opposite sense at lower latitude. Ring-region seeds select westward current at 3–8 Earth radii; they use GAMERA J rather than a separate reconstruction of the energetic-particle ring current.

Tracing stops at 2.2 Earth radii, weak current, the sampled boundary, a near return to the seed, or the integration length limit. No ionospheric connector is invented below the MHD boundary. Interpolation does not enforce divergence-free J, so these curves visualize the field without proving exact current closure.

Ionosphere and coordinates

GAMERA uses solar-magnetic Cartesian coordinates in Earth radii. REMIX FAC is mapped to the Apex ionosphere: red is outward and blue inward. FAC and current paths use the same saved model step in every snapshot. GAMERA J is measured in nA/m² and REMIX FAC in µA/m².

Run provenance

The completed hello_earth_24h run spans 9 August 2016 09:00 UTC through 10 August 2016 09:00 UTC, using MAGE/GAMERA revision 3382b1f2. The driving OMNI record has a plasma-data gap on 9 August at 09:44–12:02 UTC and an IMF gap at 09:46–11:59 UTC. Kaipy linearly interpolated both gaps; excluding the model burn-in does not remove that input-data limitation.
